What counts as an ad (keep this rule in mind — full version in README)

Anything that interrupts the program the way a commercial break does — paid spots, infomercials/shopping segments, and channel self-promos / "up next" / bumpers. In-program sponsorship counts only with a clear separable signal (on-screen "Ad"/"Sponsored" marker or a distinct break). Brand mentions and product placement stay content.

Tie-breaker: interrupts like a commercial break and has a detectable signal → ad; otherwise content. Subtype unclear after ~2s → other, move on.
